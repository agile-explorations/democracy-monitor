The administration has framed these reforms as efforts to reward high performers and improve government efficiency, goals that enjoy broad support. Many private-sector and some state government organizations use similar performance-driven retention systems. However, the private sector does not face the same risks of partisan political pressure on employees, and the simultaneous removal of grievance and review protections is harder to reconcile with a purely merit-focused rationale.
